About the Game

Three Choices is a tactical turn-based RPG built entirely in Unity. Every encounter challenges the player with meaningful decisions — each turn offers exactly three actions, each with cascading consequences. The game features a modular, data-driven architecture that makes it easy to extend enemy types, spells, and loot without touching game logic code.

Enemies are defined through event-driven data definitions, a layered status effect system handles buffs and debuffs at runtime, and combat events flow through a decoupled pipeline that keeps the UI, audio, and game logic entirely independent.

How to play

  1. Choose a virtual-credit cost and press Spin.
  2. 5+ matching relics connected horizontally or vertically form a winning cluster.
  3. Winning relics clear. New ones fall in, raising the cascade multiplier up to x5.
  4. 3+ scatters award 10 free spins, with a persistent multiplier up to x10.
Relics & paytable

Medium preset. Values multiply the spin cost and current cascade multiplier. Clusters of 12+ use the 12-symbol payout.

About the Tool

PoE Poison Simulator is a browser-based simulation tool that models Path of Exile's poison mechanics in real time. Configure your character's damage, attack rate, poison chance, duration, and scaling modifiers — the simulator tracks every active poison stack tick-by-tick to project Poison DPS and time-to-kill against real boss health pools.

Built entirely in vanilla JavaScript with no dependencies, the tool faithfully replicates PoE's stacking model: each hit applies at most one stack, stacks are tracked individually with accurate expiry, and total DPS is bounded by the engine's 32-bit integer cap at its 60 Hz tick rate. A dedicated Playtest tab lets you experience the mechanics first-hand by moving and attacking inside a live combat sandbox.
