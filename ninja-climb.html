Game Jam Project

NINJA CLIMB

Browser game

Aim. Attach. Climb.

NINJA CLIMB is a 2D vertical grappling and climbing platformer built in GameMaker. Aim with the mouse, throw your rope from solid ground, and reel in or out to work your way toward the summit. Swing, release, then land before throwing again.

GameMaker 2D Platformer Vertical Grappling Game Jam

Browser Version

Take on the climb

Click inside the game to focus it, then use your mouse and keyboard. The game pauses when it loses focus.

Open game in new tab

Open the game in a new tab for a larger view or if the embed does not work. A mouse and keyboard are required; touch controls are not available.

How to Play

Control your climb

Mouse
Aim the rope.
Left click or E
Throw the rope from solid ground, or detach it.
A / D or Left / Right arrows
Move left and right, or swing.
W / S
Reel the rope in and out.
Space or Right click
Release the rope.
R
Restart the climb.
